I'm excited to share that I’ve been awarded the [2025 Youlchon AI Young Researcher](https://aiis.snu.ac.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=eng4_3) distinction, an honor granted to only **~10 students each year** for outstanding research in **AI applications (AI+X)**.

This recognition highlights my work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, drug discovery, and biomedical modeling, where I’ve focused on building interpretable and impactful AI systems for precision medicine. From developing knowledge-aware frameworks to designing multi-modal molecular representations, the goal has always been to bridge complex data with translational insight.

Beyond the personal milestone, this award reflects the growing momentum behind integrative, domain-informed AI research in tackling real-world challenges in healthcare and life sciences. I’m deeply grateful to continue this journey.
